# # Project Name
**Wait, What?** - AI-Generated Psychological Thriller Short Film

# Project Idea
A psychological thriller short film created entirely using AI tools. The story follows two business partners, Adam and Noah, who face a massive crisis after losing a critical deal. While Adam breaks down in anger and paranoia, Noah plays the calm, supportive friend. However, a final psychological twist reveals that the betrayal came from the closest person. The film focuses on emotional manipulation, trust, and hidden intentions.

# Work Plan
Our project execution is structured into 7 comprehensive phases:
1.  **Project Briefing:** Defining the cinematic concept, psychological thriller genre, and outlining core deliverables for a 4–9 minute AI film.
2.  **Story Development & Pre-Production:** Building the narrative arc, writing the full script, and breaking it down into a detailed Scene Plan and Visual Direction manual.
3.  **Character & Visual Creation:** Generating consistent character designs (Adam, Noah, Karim), environments, and establishing key frames for each shot.
4.  **Film Production:** Converting base images into cinematic video sequences using AI tools, applying camera motion, and producing audio (dialogues, lip-sync, music).
5.  **Editing & Post-Production:** Assembling sequences into a coherent narrative, maintaining visual/lighting consistency, and optimizing color grading and sound design.
6.  **Final Film Optimization:** Reviewing pacing, scene durations, transitions, and fixing any continuity or AI-generated distortions.
7.  **Presentation and Feedback:** Preparing the final presentation covering our creative process, AI pipeline, challenges, and receiving feedback on the final cut.
